# DSA and AI Act triage

Not legal advice. Use this to decide whether specialist review is needed.

## DSA platform triage

Trigger DSA review if the service includes user accounts plus any of:

- user-generated public content;
- seller/merchant marketplace listings;
- reviews/ratings;
- ads shown to users;
- recommender systems ranking content/products;
- moderation, takedown, account restrictions, or illegal-content reporting.

Check for: terms for moderation, notice-and-action mechanism, statement of reasons, internal complaint handling, trader traceability, ad transparency, recommender transparency, minor protection, and dark-pattern restrictions.

## AI Act / GDPR automated-decision triage

Trigger AI review if the site uses:

- chatbot or synthetic-content interface;
- automated scoring/ranking with material effect;
- employment/education/credit/essential-services eligibility;
- biometric identification/categorisation, emotion recognition, or health/safety classification;
- AI vendor APIs where prompts/outputs may include personal data.

Check for: user disclosure, privacy notice coverage, vendor/DPA/transfer coverage, logging/retention, model-training use, human review/appeal, and high-risk category screening.
