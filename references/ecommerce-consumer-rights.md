# EU/UK ecommerce and consumer-rights checks

Not legal advice. Use this for sites selling goods, services, subscriptions, memberships, courses, tickets, digital content, or downloads.

## Trader identity and pre-contract information

- Legal trader identity and trading name.
- Geographic address and practical contact channel.
- Registration/company number and VAT where applicable.
- Main characteristics of goods/services/digital content.
- Total price including taxes and unavoidable fees.
- Delivery costs, delivery timing, payment methods.
- Contract formation point and accepted terms.

## Withdrawal / returns

For many distance contracts, EU consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right. Check:

- clear withdrawal explanation before purchase;
- withdrawal period and start date;
- process/contact route;
- model withdrawal form or equivalent route where applicable;
- refund timing and return-cost responsibility;
- exceptions: customized goods, sealed hygiene goods, event/date-specific services, fully performed services with consent, digital content started with required acknowledgement.

## Subscriptions and digital services

- Recurring price and billing frequency visible before payment.
- Trial-to-paid conversion disclosed.
- Cancellation is not harder than signup.
- Renewal reminders where local law/platform practice requires them.
- Digital-content functionality/compatibility/interoperability where relevant.

## Unfair practices / dark patterns

Flag hidden fees, misleading scarcity/countdowns, forced bundles, confusing cancellation, review manipulation, and prominent accept/pay buttons that obscure material terms.
