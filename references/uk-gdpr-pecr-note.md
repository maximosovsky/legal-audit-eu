# UK GDPR / PECR note

UK analysis is close to EU GDPR but not identical.

## UK-specific checks

- UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018 instead of EU GDPR alone.
- ICO is the UK supervisory authority.
- PECR governs cookies and electronic marketing.
- International transfers may use UK IDTA or UK Addendum to EU SCCs.
- Non-UK organizations targeting UK users may need a UK representative in some cases.
- Cookie/electronic-marketing guidance should be checked against current ICO practice.

## Report convention

If the site targets both EU and UK users, write findings as:

- EU: GDPR + ePrivacy Directive / member-state implementation.
- UK: UK GDPR + DPA 2018 + PECR / ICO guidance.

Do not silently say “GDPR compliant” for both regions without naming the UK variant.
